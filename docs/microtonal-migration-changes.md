## Fork update to d232b5a8 (2026-10-01)

Re-vendored after the fork's spelling and dictionary work. 12- and 24-TET are
unchanged, and so are every EDO's chord pitch classes and scale lists: the
fork now spells every EDO by fifths by default, and the adapter keeps decision
D1 with `setEdoSpelling("proportional-fallback")`.

- Note names: spellings that cross a neighbouring natural are gone, as in
  ups and downs notation (41-EDO step 1 is `C↑`, not `B#`; the 53-EDO 5/4 is
  `E↓`, not `Fb`). The 27 EDOs affected are updated in the table above;
  stored names still parse, since any spelling of a step does.
- Chord identification can name harmonic and subharmonic chords the fork
  builds per EDO (`har7`, `sub7`…), e.g. 4:5:6:7 in 19- and 72-EDO. The
  adapter looks chord types up with the EDO so these are kept.

one name.
